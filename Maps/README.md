## Current status of created products

This folder contains spatial products generated using Boosted Regression Tree (BRT) models to analyze woody vegetation transformation pathways across the Gran Chaco. The products represent spatial predictions of the probability of transition from woody vegetation to different land-cover types, based on environmental and socioecological predictors.

## The current set of products includes:

- **Projected transition maps** — spatial predictions derived from the fitted BRT models, representing the spatial distribution of each transformation pathway across the study area.

- **Spatial overlap map** — an integrated map highlighting areas where multiple transformation pathways have predicted probabilities greater than 0.75, as well as areas dominated by a single pathway.

- **Raster outputs** — spatial products provided in standard geospatial formats (e.g., GeoTIFF).

These products represent model-based spatial predictions of woody vegetation transformation pathways and their potential spatial overlap. They should be interpreted as predicted probabilities and spatial patterns of association with environmental and socioecological conditions, rather than as observed transitions or forecasts of future land-cover change.
